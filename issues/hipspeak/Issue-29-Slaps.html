The Dictionary of Slang

 

Slaps

sounds exactly how you think it does

Think: really good

In Use: 

“Ms. Lacey, I can’t stop thinking about the last part of the poem from yesterday. “The caged bird sings, with a fearful trill, of things unknown, but longed for still, and his tune is heard on the distant hill, for the caged bird sings of freedom.” I accidentally memorized it. Like damn! Angelou really went off with that. The whole poem slaps, but that part is my favourite part. When we talk about our notes today can I go first?”

- Daniel (age 13)

 

By Ms. Lacey

Disclaimer: If you were not born in this century, please do not try to incorporate this slang naturally in day-to-day use. In attempts to connect to the younger generations, you will appear beg and unc (stay tuned, we'll unpack those terms).
